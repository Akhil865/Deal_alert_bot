Deal criteria

Our alert system focuses on deals with 70% or higher advertised discounts, subject to source availability and validation.

How it works

  1. Deal sources are checked.
  2. Prices and advertised discounts are evaluated.
  3. Eligible deals are sent as Telegram alerts.
  4. Users can open the available product/deal link.

This website is for deal discovery and informational purposes. Product availability, pricing, discounts and seller information can change.
